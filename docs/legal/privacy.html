VitaMind

Privacy Policy

Effective October 5, 2026

The short version

  • VitaMind works without an account. In guest mode everything stays on your device.
  • If you create an account, your mood check-ins, symptoms, journal entries, and reminder settings are stored in your private cloud space so they can sync. Only you can access them.
  • We do not sell your data, show ads, use analytics or tracking tools, or send your entries to AI services.
  • You can delete your account and data at any time, in the app or from the web.

Who we are

VitaMind is a wellness companion app published on Google Play as “VitaMind”. In this policy, “we” and “us” mean the VitaMind developer. Questions about privacy: vitamind.support@gmail.com.

Information you give us

If you create an account

  • Email address and password. Sign-in is handled by Google Firebase Authentication. Your password is never visible to us.
  • Synced wellness entries: mood check-ins and any notes you add, symptoms (name, severity, duration, and notes), journal entries, and your reminder time and frequency.

Kept only on your device

Whether or not you have an account, these stay on your device and are never uploaded: diagnosed conditions, medications, common symptoms, wellness goals, saved health-log explanations, and your privacy settings. In guest mode, your mood, symptom, and journal entries also stay only on your device.

Information collected automatically

VitaMind does not include analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting tools, and it does not access your location, contacts, camera, or microphone. When you sign in or sync, Google Firebase processes technical information such as your IP address and device type in order to provide and secure the service. See Firebase’s privacy information.

How we use your information

Only to provide VitaMind’s features: showing your history and insights, syncing your entries between devices when you are signed in, and scheduling the reminders you turn on. We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, or marketing, and we do not sell it.

The health-log explainer runs entirely on your device and uses VitaMind’s built-in list of trusted health sources. Your text is not sent to any server or AI service.

How your information is stored and protected

  • On your device: health information is encrypted with AES-256. The key is kept in your device’s secure Android Keystore.
  • In the cloud (accounts only): entries are stored in Google Cloud Firestore, encrypted in transit and at rest by Google. Access rules allow only your signed-in account to read or change your data. Google’s servers may be located outside your country.

No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we use these safeguards to protect your information.

When information is shared

We share information only:

  • with Google, which hosts sign-in and cloud storage for VitaMind as our service provider (Firebase); and
  • if required by law, such as a valid legal request.

Reminders are scheduled on your device and are not sent through a server. Links to outside resources, such as health organizations or crisis lines, open in your browser and are covered by those sites’ own policies.

How long we keep information

We keep your information until you delete it, either by deleting your account or by deleting VitaMind data on your device as described below. When you delete your account, your account and all of its cloud data are deleted right away. Deleted data may remain in Google’s backup systems for a limited time before it is overwritten.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account and data: in the app go to Profile → Delete Account, or follow the steps on our account deletion page.
  • Delete data on this device: go to Profile → Privacy & Security → Delete Data From This Device, or uninstall the app.
  • Turn off reminders: in the app or in your device’s notification settings.
  • Access your data: view your entries in the app. To get a copy or correct something, email us.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your information. To use any of them, email vitamind.support@gmail.com.

Health information disclaimer

VitaMind is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. Please consult a healthcare professional for medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. If you are in crisis or thinking about harming yourself, call or text 988 in the US, or contact your local emergency number.

Children

VitaMind is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above. For significant changes, we will also let you know in the app.
