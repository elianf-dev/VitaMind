Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Effective October 13, 2026

This policy explains how VitaMind handles consumer health data under Washington’s My Health My Data Act (RCW 19.373). It applies to Washington residents and to anyone whose data is collected in Washington. Our general Privacy Policy covers everything else.

VitaMind is published by Elian Figueroa, whose Google Play developer name is “elianf-dev” (“we”, “us”). Contact: vitamind.support@gmail.com.

The short version

  • Guest mode: everything you log stays on your phone. We never receive it.
  • With an account: your mood check-ins, symptom logs and journal entries are stored in Google Firebase so they can sync to your account.
  • We do not sell consumer health data. We do not share it with third parties or affiliates. We don’t use it for ads and we don’t send it to any AI service.
  • You can see, get a copy of, or delete your data at any time.

1. Consumer health data we collect

If you use VitaMind as a guest, we don’t collect any consumer health data. Everything you enter is stored, encrypted, on your phone only and never sent to us.

If you create an account, we collect:

  • Mood check-ins: the mood you choose (an emoji and a word), any note you add, and the date and time.
  • Symptom logs: the symptom you type, its severity and duration, any notes, and the date and time.
  • Journal entries: the text you write, and the date and time.
  • Account identifiers linked to these entries: your email address and the account ID that Google Firebase assigns.
  • Technical information: your IP address and basic device information, which Google Firebase processes when the app signs you in or syncs.

Stays on your phone, even with an account. We never receive these: diagnosed conditions, medications, your common symptoms list, wellness goals, saved health-log explanations, and your privacy settings. Summaries, trends and health-log explanations are created on your phone from your entries and are not sent to us.

2. Why we collect it

Only to provide the features you ask for:

  • to store your entries and show your history;
  • to sync your entries to your account, so they are there when you sign in again or on another phone;
  • to sign you in and keep your account secure;
  • to answer requests you send us, such as access or deletion requests.

We don’t use consumer health data for advertising, marketing or profiling, and we don’t use it to train AI.

3. Where it comes from

  • From you: everything you type or choose in the app.
  • From Google Firebase, which records technical information (such as IP address and device type) when the app connects to sign you in or sync.

We don’t buy or receive health data about you from anyone else.

4. Who we share it with

Third parties and affiliates: none. We don’t sell or share consumer health data, and we have no affiliates.

Our service provider (processor): Google LLC, through Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore, stores and processes your account and synced entries on our behalf, under Firebase’s data processing terms, only to run sign-in and sync. Google’s servers may be outside your state or country.

When the law requires it: if we receive a valid legal order, we disclose only what the law requires.

We don’t look at your entries unless you ask us to (for example, an access or deletion request) or the law requires it.

5. Your rights and how to use them

You have the right to:

  • Confirm and access. Ask whether we collect or share your consumer health data, get a copy of it, and get a list of every third party and affiliate we have shared it with (today there are none) and how to contact them.
  • Delete. Ask us to delete your consumer health data.
  • Withdraw consent. Stop us from collecting your data. We collect it only to provide the account and sync you ask for, so to stop, use guest mode or delete your account (below). If we ever ask for your consent for something else, you can withdraw it at any time.

Delete it yourself

  • In the app: Profile → Delete Account. This deletes your account, all your synced entries in the cloud, and that account’s entries on the phone you use.
  • On the web: https://elianf-dev.github.io/VitaMind/legal/delete-account.html (sign in and delete).
  • Cloud data is deleted right away. Copies may stay in Google’s backup systems for up to six months before they are overwritten, and are not used in that time.
  • On your phone: Profile → Privacy & Security → Delete Data From This Device removes all VitaMind data from this phone, for every profile used on it, including guest data. Entries already synced to an account stay in the cloud until you delete the account.
  • Data stays on a phone where you used VitaMind until you delete it there or uninstall the app. Signing out doesn’t remove it. Uninstalling removes all VitaMind data from that phone, including guest data.

Send us a request

Email vitamind.support@gmail.com from the email address you use with VitaMind, with the subject “Consumer health data request” and say what you want (access, copy, deletion). You don’t need to create an account to make a request. We may ask you to confirm your identity, for example by replying from your account email, and we use that information only to check it’s you. If you only used guest mode, we have no data about you. Your data is on your phone, and you can delete it in Profile → Privacy & Security → Delete Data From This Device or by uninstalling.

Requests are free (up to two access requests in 12 months). We respond within 45 days. If we need more time, we’ll tell you within those 45 days, and we can extend once by up to 45 more days.

Appeal

If we decline your request, reply to our answer with the subject “Appeal”. We’ll answer within 45 days in writing and explain what we decided and why. If we deny your appeal, you can contact the Washington State Attorney General: www.atg.wa.gov/file-complaint, or 1-800-551-4636 (in Washington) or 206-464-6684.

6. Changes

If we want to collect new categories of consumer health data, use it for a new purpose, or share it, we will update this policy and ask for your consent first.
