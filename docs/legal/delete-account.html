Delete your VitaMind account and data

You can delete your VitaMind account at any time. Deleting your account permanently removes it and the data stored with it.

Delete your account here

Sign in with your VitaMind email and password to delete your account right away.

Can’t sign in?

Email vitamind.support@gmail.com from the address you use with VitaMind, with the subject “Delete my VitaMind account”. We confirm the request comes from that address, delete the account, and reply within 30 days.

Delete it yourself in the app

  1. Open VitaMind and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Enter your password and tap Delete Account to confirm.

You need an internet connection so your cloud data can be removed.

What is deleted

  • Your VitaMind account: your email address and sign-in details.
  • All data stored with your account in the cloud: mood check-ins and notes, symptoms, journal entries, and reminder settings.
  • If you delete from the app, this account’s data on that device as well.

What is kept

  • We do not keep any of your account data after deletion. Deleted data may remain in Google’s backup systems for a limited time before it is overwritten.
  • If you delete your account on this page or by email, VitaMind data on your phone is not removed remotely. To remove it, uninstall the app or go to Profile → Privacy & Security → Delete Data From This Device.

Using VitaMind as a guest?

Guest mode has no account, and your data never leaves your device. To delete it, go to Profile → Privacy & Security → Delete Data From This Device, or uninstall VitaMind.

See the VitaMind Privacy Policy for more about how your information is handled.
